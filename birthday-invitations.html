Why a link beats a group text

Group texts get messy fast — half the replies are "I think I can make it," the other half go unanswered until the day before. A single invitation link keeps everything in one place: the details, the map link, the "add to calendar" button, and a running tally of who's actually coming.

It works for any age or scale — a kid's birthday party, a milestone 30th, or a surprise party where you need to coordinate quietly without a group text tipping off the guest of honor.

What to include

  • The basics — who, what time, where, and how long. "Starting at 3pm" is clearer than just a date.
  • A dress code or theme line, if there is one — "dress code: festive" or "come in your best '90s outfit."
  • An RSVP deadline, especially if you're ordering food or a cake based on headcount.
  • Anything guests should bring — a dish for a potluck, swimsuits for a pool party, etc. — in the message field.
