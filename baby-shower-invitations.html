Why hosts use a link for baby showers

Baby shower guest lists tend to mix generations — some guests are comfortable with apps, some aren't, and nobody wants to explain how to use one over the phone. A plain link sidesteps that entirely: open it, tap "Count me in," done. It also means the parent-to-be isn't fielding "did you get my text?" messages for weeks — the live tally shows exactly who's coming.

What to include

  • Whether it's a surprise — if the parent-to-be doesn't know, say so clearly at the top of the message so nobody slips.
  • A registry link or note, if there is one — drop it in the message field so guests don't have to ask.
  • Theme or color scheme, if guests are dressing to match or there's a "no gifts, just diapers" ask.
  • An RSVP deadline — most showers involve food and games planned around headcount, so a clear cutoff date helps.
