Why couples send digital wedding invitations

Paper suites are beautiful, but they're slow — ordering, addressing, mailing, and waiting weeks for replies. A link-based invitation goes out the moment you're ready, works for guests across every time zone, and gives you a live headcount instead of a stack of reply cards to track by hand.

It also works well as a save-the-date or an engagement party invite before you commit to a full paper suite for the wedding itself — you can always print formal invitations later and use the digital version for RSVP tracking either way.

Wedding invitation wording, a few ways

There's no single "correct" wording — it depends on who's hosting and how formal the day is. A few starting points:

Together with their families,
Jess Martinez & Sam Lee
request the pleasure of your company
as they exchange vows.
We're getting married! Come celebrate with us —
Jess & Sam

If parents are hosting and paying, it's traditional to lead with their names ("Mr. & Mrs. Martinez request the honor of your presence..."). If the couple is hosting themselves, leading with both of your names is standard and simpler. Keep the essentials — who, what, when, where, and how to RSVP — easy to find no matter which tone you pick.
