Looking for something simpler than Evite?

RSVP Dove is a free way to make an invitation and share it as one link. No account to create, nothing for your guests to sign up for, and no ads on the page they open.

What people usually want from an "Evite alternative"

When people go looking for an alternative to the big invitation sites, it's usually one of a few things: guests shouldn't have to make an account just to say yes or no, the free tier shouldn't feel limited, and the page itself shouldn't be cluttered with things unrelated to the party.

RSVP Dove is built around those three points specifically:

  • No account for guests. Open the link, tap "Count me in" or "Can't make it," done. There's nothing to sign up for.
  • No account for you either. There's no login, no password, no app to install. Fill in your event, get a link, send it.
  • Nothing on the invitation but the invitation. No ads or unrelated promotions on the page your guests open.

How it works

  1. Fill in your event details — title, host, date, time, location, and an optional message.
  2. Pick from 12 designed themes, or upload your own photo as the background.
  3. Copy your link and send it however you'd text or email a friend.
  4. Guests open it, RSVP right there, and you see a live tally of who's coming.

If you'd rather not track RSVPs on the page at all, link-only mode works too — guests tap a button that opens a pre-filled text or email reply to you, so there's no database involved at all.

Good for

Birthdays, dinner parties, baby showers, graduations, holiday get-togethers — anything where you'd otherwise be stuck coordinating over a group text or chasing people for a headcount.
