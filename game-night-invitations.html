Why not just use the group chat?

You can, but half the replies get buried under unrelated messages and you end up re-asking "wait, who's actually coming?" an hour before. A link keeps the headcount visible to everyone — useful when you're deciding between a 4-player game and a 6-player one, or whether to order enough snacks for eight.

What to include

  • What you're playing — board games, cards, video games, trivia — so people know what to expect (and whether to bring their own controller).
  • Whether to bring a game — if you want people to bring their own favorites, say so in the message.
  • Snacks or BYOB — worth a line if you're not providing everything yourself.
  • A rough headcount cutoff — some games need a minimum or maximum number of players, so knowing who's in ahead of time actually matters here more than most parties.
